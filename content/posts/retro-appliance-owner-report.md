Every retro appliance guide, including earlier versions of ours, tells you the same story: the Smeg is the beautifully engineered original, the Haden is the sensible copy, and you are choosing between build quality and budget. So we went and counted what owners actually complain about — and the story did not survive contact with the reviews.

This is the second in our **Owner Reports** series. Instead of repeating marketing copy, we read verified owner reviews at scale, tally what people actually say, and publish the pattern — including the parts that contradict what we ourselves wrote.

## What we did

We read **194 unique owner reviews** across **four best-selling retro appliances** on Amazon — the Smeg and Haden 2-slice toasters, and the Smeg and Haden electric kettles — then counted how often specific themes appeared. We deliberately read across the whole star range: most helpful, and reviews at each star level from one to five, so criticism is represented rather than buried.

**An important note on the numbers.** Because we sampled evenly across star ratings, our sample is *not* a representative slice of all owners. The real distribution is far more positive — these products average 4.3 to 4.4 stars. Percentages below describe **the reviews we read**, split into 119 critical (1–3 star) and 75 positive (4–5 star). That is deliberate: it is the right way to find *what goes wrong*, and the wrong way to estimate *how often*. For overall satisfaction, use the star averages, which cover every owner.

## Finding 1: the premium does not buy you even toast

This is the result that made us rewrite our own toaster guide.

**Uneven browning was the single most common complaint for both toasters** — appearing in **47% of the 32 critical Smeg reviews we read**, and **43% of the 28 critical Haden reviews**. The complaint is near-identical at $180 and at $45: one slot toasts darker than the other, or one side of the slice browns and the other does not.

> "I tried two of these, neither one worked well. One side did not toast. I had to turn the toast around to toast both sides. Beautiful toaster tho." — Amazon reviewer, 3 stars, Smeg

> "It looks unbelievably pretty but does an uneven job with toast." — Amazon reviewer, 3 stars, Smeg

> "Eh, looks good but it doesn't toast evenly. It is quick though." — Amazon reviewer, 3 stars, Haden

We had previously written that the Smeg "browns evenly across both slots." Our own data does not support that, and we have corrected the guide. This is the whole point of reading reviews at scale rather than repeating received wisdom.

## Finding 2: the durability gap runs the wrong way

You would expect the four-times-more-expensive toaster to last longer. In our sample it drew *more* failure complaints, not fewer.

Language about something breaking, stopping, dying or needing replacement appeared in **41% of the critical Smeg toaster reviews we read**, against **21% for the Haden**.

> "One day our toaster stopped working though product was only two years in. Smeg would not refund nor replace." — Amazon reviewer, 2 stars

> "This broke within 9 months." — Amazon reviewer, 2 stars

One important caveat: several Smeg reviewers raise doubts about whether they received a genuine unit, and counterfeit or grey-market stock would inflate this figure. Buy from a seller you trust. But even allowing for that, nothing in our reading supports treating the Smeg as the more durable purchase.

## Finding 3: retro kettles rust, and nobody tells you

This is the finding we did not expect and have not seen in any competing guide.

Interior rust, corrosion or discolouration appeared in **23% of the 30 critical Haden kettle reviews we read** and **14% of the 29 critical Smeg kettle reviews** — and in almost none of the positive ones. Owners describe rust forming inside the body or around the lid rim after months of use.

> "Had this kettle for 2 years and realized the inside was very rusted. Super cute and functions well though." — Amazon reviewer, 2 stars, Haden

> "Worked well and was beautiful on my counter but after a few weeks I noticed that it was corroded inside." — Amazon reviewer, 2 stars, Smeg

> "Kettle is starting to form blackness around the rim where the lid sits." — Amazon reviewer, 2 stars, Haden

A related, smaller thread: a handful of owners of both kettles were unhappy to find plastic components inside the water path. If that matters to you, check the interior construction before buying.

## Finding 4: kettle buyers argue with the price more than anything else

For both kettles, value language — "worth it", "overpriced", "for the money", "pricey" — was the **most common critical theme**, ahead of any functional issue: **34% of critical Smeg kettle reviews** and **30% of critical Haden kettle reviews**.

That is a different pattern from the toasters, where function dominated. It suggests the kettle is where the retro premium feels hardest to justify — it boils water, and every owner knows a $25 kettle also boils water.

The Haden kettle carries its own version of this: **30% of its critical reviews** describe it as feeling cheap, flimsy or lightweight. So the two kettles fail differently — one feels too expensive for what it does, the other feels too light for what it costs.

## What this means before you buy

**Do not pay the Smeg premium expecting better toast or a longer life.** Our reading does not support either. Pay it if you want the specific object on your counter every morning — that is a legitimate reason, and the owners who bought it for that are consistently happy.

**If you mainly want the look, the Haden is the rational buy.** Same headline complaint, proportionally fewer durability complaints in our sample, at a quarter of the price. Accept that it will feel lighter in the hand.

**For any retro kettle, dry the interior and descale.** It is the one specific habit our data suggests actually prevents a real problem, and it costs nothing.

**Expect to learn your toaster.** Given how universal the uneven-browning complaint is across price points, plan on a few weeks of adjusting the dial and rotating slices, whatever you buy.
